# Sales and traction

Pixel Hopper is **free** on itch.io (<https://the-protagonist07.itch.io/pixel-hopper>). It was started as a trial to learn how publishing platforms and players respond, and may grow
into something with a business model later. This folder holds what is needed to decide that with facts.

## Where the numbers come from
The itch.io dashboard for the game shows views, browser plays, downloads and followers, plus where visitors came from. Record the numbers regularly in [metrics-log.md](metrics-log.md)
(for example every Sunday and after each update), so trends and the effect of each update are visible.

## What to look at
| Signal | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Views vs browser plays | Does the page make people start the game? (cover, title, description) |
| Plays after an update | Did the devlog post bring people back? |
| Comments | Which stage do players reach, and where do they get stuck? (feeds KI-005 and the roadmap) |
| Followers | Is there an audience for regular updates? |
| Where visitors came from | Which post or site worked (Reddit, X, itch.io browse) |

## Plans (see ROADMAP for details)
- Score, timer and best times to give a reason to replay, then a leaderboard.
- Mobile stores: technically possible with Unity (Android first as a soft launch); profit is unlikely without retention and user acquisition, so the first step is only to measure interest.
  Details: [docs/ROADMAP.md](../docs/ROADMAP.md) section 4.
- Monetisation ideas, none started: optional "pay what you want" on itch.io, a premium character or world pack, ads in a mobile version.

## Decision rule (starting point)
Keep investing while each update measurably increases plays or comments. Revisit the plan after about a month of metrics.
