## Description (paste into the page, itch supports Markdown)

**Pixel Hopper is a bite-sized pixel platformer built for portrait play.**

The view is narrow on purpose: you only see a few tiles ahead, so every gap, spike and enemy is a quick decision. Reach the flag at the end of each stage, grab as many coins as you can, and see if you can clear all 10.

### Features
- **10 stages, 10 worlds:** Sunny Meadow, Sunset Run, Deep Cave, Frozen Peaks, Dusty Desert, Midnight, Lava Fields and more, each with its own sky, mountains and mood
- **Easy to learn, tricky to finish:** stages ramp from gentle hops to spike runs and enemy gauntlets
- **Stomp enemies** from above, dodge spikes and pits, and chain coins for a rising chime
- **3 lives per stage**, instant retry, no menus in the way
- **Chiptune music and sound effects**
- **Plays right in your browser**, on desktop or phone. No download, no sign-up

### Controls
| Device | Move | Jump |
|---|---|---|
| Keyboard | A / D or arrow keys | Space, W or Up (hold = higher jump) |
| Gamepad | Left stick or D-pad | A / bottom button |
| Touch | Left side of the screen | Right side of the screen |

Press **M** to mute, **H** for the help dialog.

### Tips
- Hold jump for a higher hop, tap it for a short one.
- Hold jump when you land on an enemy for a bigger bounce.
- Coin arcs usually trace a safe jump path.

### About
Made with Unity. All art, music and sound effects are built entirely in code, with no imported art or audio packs.

I'd love to hear **which stage you reached** and where you got stuck. Drop a comment!
